Shared gear for four people on the Kepler Track. Personal gear is in the trip
plan.

## Food

- Breakfast: porridge, milk powder, coffee
- Lunch: crackers, cheese, salami, fruit
- Dinner: dehydrated meals, rice, hot chocolate
- Snacks: scroggin, muesli bars

## Before leaving

1. Check the track and weather forecasts.
2. Pack the beacon and spare batteries.
3. Leave intentions with someone at home.
